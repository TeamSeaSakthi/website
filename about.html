Our Institution

Kumaraguru Institutions

For forty years, Kumaraguru Institutions have promoted scientific thinking, analysis, and inquiry in education, research, and innovation. Committed to technology for ecological resilience, we champion sustainability, poised to make a significant positive impact on the environment.

The Competition

Monaco Energy Boat Challenge

The Monaco Energy Boat Challenge is a globally recognized competition that brings together the brightest student innovators in sustainable marine mobility. Organized by the Yacht Club de Monaco, the event challenges teams to design and race clean energy-powered boats. Team Sea Sakthi proudly represents India for the fifth consecutive year.
