CURRENT EDITION

Yali 5.0

Building on four generations of innovation, Yali 5.0 represents our most ambitious vessel yet. Designed for the Monaco Energy Boat Challenge 2026, it pushes the boundaries of clean energy propulsion, advanced materials, and intelligent marine systems — carrying India's flag on the global stage for the fifth consecutive year.

YALI 5.0 Features

Precision Propulsion

Our 15 kW axial flux motor delivers instant response with maximum efficiency, like having a sports car that's also great on fuel.

Eco-Smart Cockpit

Built using balsa wood and flax fiber from nature — super-strong, lightweight, kind to environment, absorbs bumps for smoother ride.

Advanced AR Navigation

AR glasses show speed, navigation, and warnings right in front of your eyes — like a video game HUD for real boating.

Power Distribution Unit

Smart power management with 60% less wiring, waterproof IP67 rated, built tough for marine conditions.

Advanced Safety System

Constantly watches for electrical problems and instantly responds — like having a trained safety expert on board.

Smart Performance Tracking

Monitors energy use to system health — a fitness tracker for the boat, no technical expertise required.
